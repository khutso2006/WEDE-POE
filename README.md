## 3. Project Overview

Elevated Steps is an online sneaker retail website designed to provide customers with a convenient and user-friendly way to view and purchase stylish sneakers. The organisation focuses on providing fashionable, comfortable and affordable sneakers for both men and women.

The website is designed to give customers access to information about available sneakers, prices and product categories. It also provides customers with different ways to interact with the business through the contact and inquiry sections.

The website aims to create a simple and professional online shopping experience while presenting the products in an attractive and organised manner.

---

## 4. Website Goals and Objectives

The main goal of the Elevated Steps website is to create an attractive and easy-to-use online platform where customers can explore sneakers and obtain information about the products offered by the organisation.

### Objectives

The primary purpose of creating the Elevated Steps website is to establish a strong online presence and provide customers with an easy and convenient way to purchase sneakers.
Website Goals
•	Increase brand awareness.
•	Expand the business by reaching customers across South Africa.
•	Sell sneakers online 24 hours a day.
•	Display available products with detailed descriptions and prices.
•	Promote new arrivals, discounts, and special offers.
•	Build customer trust through a professional online presence.
•	Improve communication between the business and customers.
•	Increase sales and customer satisfaction.


## 5. Key Features and Functionality

The Elevated Steps website includes the following features:

### Home Page

The home page introduces customers to Elevated Steps and provides an attractive welcome section for the business.

### Navigation Bar

The navigation bar allows users to easily move between the different sections of the website.

### Products Page

The products page displays the sneakers available from Elevated Steps. Customers can view product images, names and prices.

### Men's Sneakers

This section displays sneakers suitable for men and provides relevant product information.

### Women's Sneakers

This section displays sneakers suitable for women and provides relevant product information.

### Shopping Cart

The shopping cart section is designed to allow customers to view products they have selected for purchase.

### Inquiry Page

The inquiry page allows customers to submit questions about products, prices, sizes, delivery, returns and other services.

### Contact Page

The contact page provides customers with information on how they can contact Elevated Steps.

### Product Information

Products include information such as the sneaker name, brand and price to help customers make informed decisions.

## 7. Part 1 Details

Part 1 focuses on establishing the foundation of the Elevated Steps website.

The website structure was created using HTML. The project includes multiple pages that allow customers to navigate through the website and access information about the organisation and its products.

The main pages developed include:

* Home
* About Us
* Products
* Shopping Cart
* Inquiries
* Contact Us

The website uses a navigation structure that allows users to move between the different pages.

The product section contains sneaker images, product names, brands and prices. An inquiry form was also included to allow customers to submit questions to the organisation.

The project files are maintained in a private GitHub repository, where changes are regularly committed using descriptive commit messages.
